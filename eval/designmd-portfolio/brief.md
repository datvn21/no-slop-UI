# Portfolio trial brief

Synthetic fixture for testing the skill, not a real person's work.

- A motion designer needs a portfolio that lets a visitor inspect three projects and contact them.
- Project names and descriptions below are fictional studies. No clients, outcomes, testimonials, photos, videos, or metrics are supplied.
- Contact uses a test address only; no message is sent from this page.
- The page must work at phone, tablet, desktop, wide widths, keyboard, and reduced motion.
- Reference inspected: [DesignMD's Portfolio Dev Full-Stack](https://designmd.app/library/portfolio-dev-full-stack/), 2026-10-30. Transfer project-first hierarchy and restrained timing. Reject its prescribed testimonials, pricing, and 3D hero.

Projects: **Nếp** — a typographic rhythm study; **Dải** — a sequence of moving fields; **Khoảng** — a study of pause and negative space.
